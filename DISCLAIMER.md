# Disclaimer

Indian Trading Agent is open-source research and decision-support software.

It does not provide personalised investment advice, portfolio-management services, brokerage, exchange connectivity, custody, tax advice, legal advice or guaranteed returns. Outputs can be wrong, incomplete, delayed or unsuitable for a user's circumstances.

Market data, exchange rules, taxes, transaction charges, settlement, short-selling/product eligibility, margins and broker behaviour can change. Verify material facts with current authoritative sources and your broker/exchange before acting.

The software does not place orders. Any separate execution system is outside this repository and must apply its own controls, authentication, compliance and risk checks.
