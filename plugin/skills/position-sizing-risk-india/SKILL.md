# Position Sizing & Per-Trade Risk

Two constraints matter independently:
1. how much can be lost if the stop is reached,
2. how much capital/notional can be concentrated in the position.

Use the smaller quantity from stop-risk sizing and exposure-cap sizing. Show which constraint binds.

Also discuss when relevant:
- gap risk means realised loss can exceed stop-defined risk,
- liquidity/impact may reduce size,
- lot/product constraints,
- correlated existing positions,
- event risk around results/corporate actions.

Do not move a technically valid stop just to increase quantity. Do not imply a stop guarantees the modelled loss.
